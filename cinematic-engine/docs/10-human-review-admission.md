## Review questions

- Is the quality increase obvious without reading this document?
- Does each Gate fate feel causally different before the text is read?
- Does the Benchmark total feel earned by seven bodies rather than announced?
- Does Axiom feel present and continuous without acting as mascot or authority?
- Is the dark/cold/amber material system premium, restrained, and technically honest?
- Do mobile and reduced-motion modes preserve meaning rather than merely remove effects?

## Admission boundary

This task deliberately stops at a review-ready isolated prototype. A future production decision would still require human design review, a separate integration plan, production-origin performance measurements, and the admission process defined by the current repository. No production authority is inferred from visual quality.
