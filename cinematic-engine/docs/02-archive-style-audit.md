# 02 — Archive and style audit

The archive audit treated the supplied ChatGPT and Twitter/X material as creative-direction evidence only. Raw archive content was not admitted as memory, product truth, technical proof, or Genesis history.

## Coverage

- ChatGPT: 578 conversations, 55,940 normalized messages, 51.7 million characters, 7,203 DAT files (about 5.1 GB), including 4,045 JPEG and 2,650 PNG assets.
- Twitter/X: 7,831 authored records and about 254 MB of media; 2,959 style-relevant records were ranked for review.
- `WHY.txt`: read in full and synchronized with the task.
- Genesis: all 12 admitted eras, 14 admitted milestones, and the earlier v01/v02 cinematic frames were inspected without rewriting admission history.

## Stable taste signal

The strongest repeated preference is grounded future infrastructure rather than generic cyberpunk: dark blue-black material, cold signal light, restrained archival amber, physically plausible boundaries, large-scale depth, clear hierarchy, and controlled glow. Repeated negative signals were flat UI diagrams, fantasy-magic Gate imagery, cracked or unstable authority objects, glow-only treatment, abstract-only composition, and decorative particles.

## Genesis and episodic evolution

The requested WandaVision reference was used as a directing principle, not as copied imagery: each world changes material, camera, rhythm, and physical law while one continuity trace and one governance contract remain stable. Field is possibility, Gate is consequence, Evidence is assembly. Axiom carries witness continuity through each episode.

## Design translation

- Cold cyan = active signal and candidate trace.
- Restrained gold = admitted history, evidence, and durable receipt.
- Green = verified release consequence.
- Amber = unresolved review orbit.
- Red = withheld or failed-closed boundary.
- A persistent hexagonal trace is the object that survives world transitions.
