# 03 — Frozen production versus cinematic candidate

## What frozen production already does well

- Uses the real public Gate and preserves SHOW/PROCEED, REVIEW/NEEDS_REVIEW, BLOCK/SILENCE mapping.
- Hash-verifies the only published SHOW candidate.
- Preserves release-decision receipts and avoids post-Gate mutation.
- Runs the real seven-category Benchmark analyzer.
- Preserves EN/UA/RU, reduced motion, no-JS meaning, Genesis history, and Axiom semantics.

## What the audit found visually weak

The functional baseline composes correct panels and routes, but its dominant experience is still a sequence of screens. The candidate, boundary, consequence, evidence, and receipt are explained by copy and card state more than by physical behavior. Axiom is primarily a corner badge. Benchmark values appear in an instrument layout, but seven evidence bodies do not visibly converge before the total settles.

## Candidate change

The prototype replaces page-to-page discontinuity with one persistent Canvas world and semantic DOM instruments. It gives the candidate mass, gives the authority boundary resistance, and makes each Gate outcome causally distinct. The Benchmark score is withheld until seven real category bodies assemble. Axiom moves into the world and reacts to state without acquiring authority.

No existing production route is replaced. Direct frozen screenshots and prototype screenshots are captured at matching desktop dimensions in the rendered review package.
