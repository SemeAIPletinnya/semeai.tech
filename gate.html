THE PRODUCTION CONTRACT

One decision. Two semantic layers. One release boundary.

SHOW / PROCEED releases only the exact evaluated candidate.

REVIEW / NEEDS_REVIEW withholds candidate content and routes the decision out of band.

BLOCK / SILENCE denies release and execution while preserving audit. It is not deletion.

ERROR fails closed. No warning, fallback, or replacement answer is released.
